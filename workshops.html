A Day With Us

How a workshop unfolds

Morning · 01

Market & mise en place

Taste the produce, learn what's in season and set up your station like a pro.

Late morning · 02

Technique deep-dive

The chef demonstrates each technique slowly, then you repeat it at your counter.

Afternoon · 03

Cook the full menu

Three to four dishes, cooked in pairs, with the chef coaching every step.

Late afternoon · 04

Long-table lunch

Everyone sits down together to eat, toast and swap stories — then pack up leftovers.
