About Culina Studio

Rooted in flavour, raised by community.

We began with one counter, six stools and a pot of ragù. Twelve years later Culina Studio is a home for curious cooks — a place where chefs teach patiently, ingredients come from people we know, and every student leaves with dinner and a new skill.

Our Story

A kitchen that feels like someone's home

Meera grew up cooking beside her grandmother in Hyderabad; Daniel spent a decade on the line in restaurant kitchens. They met teaching a Sunday bread class and realised they wanted the same thing: a place where people learn by doing, not by watching.

So in 2014 they rented a tiny ground-floor space, built one long counter and opened the doors. Classes filled by word of mouth. Today the studio has three teaching kitchens, fourteen resident chefs and a community of more than twelve thousand cooks.

"We don't want you to copy a recipe. We want you to understand it, so it becomes yours."

— Meera Rao, Co-founder
Our Journey

From six stools to a full studio

The milestones that shaped Culina Studio — each one cooked up with our students.

2014

The first counter

One room, one long counter and six stools. Our first class — fresh pasta — sold out in two days.

2017

Chef's Table launches

We added private tasting dinners, letting guests cook alongside our chefs for a ten-course evening.

2020

Kitchens at home

When doors closed, we went online — live classes and a recipe library that now holds 400+ replays.

2024

Three kitchens & a pastry lab

We moved to Banjara Hills with three teaching kitchens and a dedicated pastry and bread lab.

How We Teach

Our four-step approach

  1. 01

    Taste first

    Every class opens with the finished dish, so you know exactly what you're aiming for.

  2. 02

    Learn the why

    The chef explains the technique behind each step — heat, timing, seasoning — before you start.

  3. 03

    Cook it yourself

    Your own station, your own tools. The chef moves between counters, correcting and encouraging.

  4. 04

    Share the table

    We sit down together to eat, swap notes and take home recipe cards and video replays.
